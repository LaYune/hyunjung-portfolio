01 / The business question

The commercial brief.

A category decision requires a clear hypothesis, a measurable baseline and an honest account of what the data cannot tell us.

Myprotein has reported stronger branded unit volumes, while THG Nutrition reports growth at the broader segment level. That is business context, not evidence of UK demand for crunchy inclusions. The decision is whether chewable texture adds enough consumer value to justify a commercially viable product experiment.

Reading rule: segment growth explains business context; SKU ingredients show documented format differences; neither estimates UK demand or the treatment effect. The primary question is whether added inclusions justify a sensory, behavioural and commercial pilot.

Positioning inherited from the IE Business School coursework: the consumer wants achievable progress, and product credibility must support that emotional benefit. This portfolio tests the commercial implications rather than repeating brand-house or logo exercises. Original Choose You campaign context ↗.

One texture hypothesis. Two comparison roles.

Moving from desk research to a decision with proposed, draft, pre-registration-oriented validation criteria. No UK consumer experiments were conducted for this portfolio.

A · existing market comparator

Myprotein already lists a Genmaicha powder with green tea and brown rice. Use as a market-positioning comparator, not as the causal control for texture.

H1PRIMARY / SENSORY

Texture improves overall liking.

Among UK adult participants, the grain-flavoured protein prototype with chewable inclusions will receive a higher mean blind overall-liking score than the same grain-flavoured base without inclusions.

Identification limit: the experiment estimates the effect of adding inclusions to a matched base; it does not by itself prove a pure mechanistic texture effect because inclusions may also change appearance, energy or sweetness.

Independent variable Inclusion present / absentPrimary outcome 9-point overall likingContrast Paired B − C
H0, H1 and analysis protocol +

H₀: Mean paired difference Δ ≤ 0. H₁: Δ > 0. Participants evaluate both matched formulations in counterbalanced order, without branding or packaging. Record paired mean difference, 95% confidence interval and a paired test appropriate to distribution and preregistered assumptions.

Illustrative advancement rule: point-estimate lift of at least 0.5 point on a 9-point scale AND the two-sided 95% confidence interval for Δ entirely above zero. The 0.5-point minimum is a planning assumption, not an observed consumer benchmark. Estimate the paired-difference SD in a pilot and compute the required sample size before confirmatory recruitment.

Interpretation: a 95% interval wholly above zero supports a positive direction, but does not establish that the true effect exceeds 0.5. That stronger claim would require the interval lower bound to exceed the pre-set practical threshold.

NOT TESTED
H2SECONDARY / BEHAVIOUR

Preference converts to paid trial.

When eligible UK consumers receive a comparable, randomly assigned offer at the same net price and exposure, the textured proposition will have a higher completed paid-trial conversion than its matched smooth alternative.

Outcome Orders / unique eligible exposed usersDesign Randomised offerGuardrail Refunds / channel mix
H0, H2 and analysis protocol +

H₀: Paid-trial conversion difference ≤ 0. H₂: Difference > 0. Compare conversion denominators within the same traffic window, price, promotion and channel, with unique assignment per person. Report effect in percentage points with a confidence interval.

Decision rule: require positive evidence of incremental paid demand and a business-relevant minimum effect derived from actual margin and acquisition costs. A numerical lift target and sample size are not yet set because baseline conversion and viable unit economics are unknown. Click-through or stated intent is not paid demand.

NOT TESTED

Incremental demand covers its costs.

A limited textured-SKU pilot can produce positive incremental contribution after variable costs, displaced incumbent contribution, refunds and one-time pilot costs are accounted for.

01

Discover the occasion

12–18 purposively recruited UK adults across regular protein users, occasional users and protein-curious non-buyers. Interviews plus a 3-day usage diary distinguish breakfast, snack and post-workout needs.

Qualitative exploration only; no representative percentage estimates.
02

Run a blinded sensory test

Indicative n ≈ 90, not powered yet. Randomise and counterbalance matched C/B serving order; include Genmaicha only as a separate exploratory benchmark. Collect 9-point liking, texture satisfaction, aftertaste and rejection reasons.

Run a power calculation before any confirmatory claim; standardise temperature, sweetness and protein where feasible.

Only after safety and formulation checks: randomised concept offers or limited controlled SKU test. Track completed purchase, net incremental contribution, refund rate and a mature 90-day repeat cohort.

11 / Strategic recommendation

A decision, not a launch pitch.

A conditional recommendation. This is an evidence-led test plan, not a launch approval or a measured UK demand forecast.

01 / H1 fails

Stop the texture claim.

If the matched-base H1 test finds no meaningful incremental liking for inclusions, do not claim a texture advantage. Consider simplifying the concept or exploring an ordinary grain-flavour extension in a separate direct test.

02 / H1 passes, H2 does not

Evidence before expansion.

If sensory liking improves but a randomised equal-price offer does not improve completed paid trials, pause. Reformulate the offer, occasion or price rather than inferring willingness to pay from a taste vote.

03 / H1 + H2 + H3 evidence

Pilot only after all gates.

If verified sensory and paid-demand evidence is favourable, proceed only with food safety, shelf-life, allergen and production checks, positive incremental pilot contribution and planned 90-day cohort monitoring before scaling.

THE INVESTMENT GATE

Do not approve scale-up today. Validate the H1 matched formulation contrast, H2 completed paid trial and H3 observed incremental contribution, with allergen, operational and 90-day repeat guardrails. The illustrative model cannot substitute for THG internal costs or actual consumer behaviour.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE DOES NOT PROVE

Brand resonance is not incrementality

The campaign's emotional relevance cannot by itself establish incremental purchases, healthier retention or higher contribution margin.

Proposed test: compare a campaign-consistent product-selection page with a business-as-usual page, randomised within eligible traffic. Record product comprehension, qualified purchase conversion and contribution per exposed visitor.

Guardrail: do not claim a campaign effect from the February 2025 YouGov brand metrics: they predate the Q1 2026 campaign.

Decision implication. Preserve the inclusive “Choose You” emotional promise while testing whether clearer ingredient guidance, product-use explanations and quality evidence improve a consumer's ability to act on that promise. Treat the effect as an unverified hypothesis until measured.

My strategic observation

Both platforms recast fitness as a personal, non-punitive experience. However, Myprotein must make that emotional promise concrete through product trust, easy nutritional choices and a repeatable cross-channel buying experience; lululemon’s model is mediated by apparel innovation, embodied product experience and community. This cross-category analogy can suggest a hypothesis — it cannot transfer lululemon’s earnings growth to Myprotein.

Measurement framework / What I would test with appropriate access
01 · Brand lift

Track aided awareness, message recall, consideration, brand association (“fitness on my terms”), and preference. Use exposed/control cohorts with market, season and media-frequency controls.

Measure 90/180-day repeat rates by acquisition cohort, second-purchase timing, cross-channel recognition and retention contribution. Avoid inferring long-run loyalty from one quarter’s revenue.

Data contract

Four rules prevent misleading totals

01 / Declare grain. Quarter-level THG segment revenue is a different fact from H1 Optimum Nutrition brand growth.

02 / Label metrics. A rate is non-additive; summing growth percentages is invalid.

03 / Control period overlap. FY2025 and Q1–Q4 2025 describe overlapping time ranges and cannot both enter the same monetary total.

04 / Respect missingness. Undisclosed ON volume growth remains null, not zero.
