# Evidence-led Portfolio V3.4 | Recruiter-first revision

**Date:** 19 November 2026. New page: `/v3.4/`. V3.3 remains unchanged.

## Why this version exists
V3.3 showed substantial evidence but asked a recruiter to read many full cases before understanding (a) which junior role this candidate is targeting, (b) what they personally delivered, and (c) how a finding relates to a next decision. Repeated limitation statements also displaced stronger evidence.

## Changes
1. Rewrote the first screen for Junior Market, Commercial, Category and Consumer Insight roles. Did not claim an established job title in commercial analytics.
2. Added a 30-second recruiter brief with three evidence-backed proof examples: Pathfinder (UK voluntary internship, real submitted report and founder planning), Myprotein (independent 17-product research and **untested** UK texture proposition), and education-sector paid work (diagnosis, recommendation and follow-up).
3. Made actual output versus proposed decision prominent. For Myprotein: test before launch; UK purchase and sensory tests have not occurred.
4. Added the specific Pathfinder analytical diagnosis (brand name ambiguity). This is supported by the original internship evidence, not claimed digital marketing uplift.
5. Labelled Lidl and FitFlop as comparison-method portfolios, without inventing a missing empirical sales finding. Added **explicitly proposed** next analyses, not completed work.
6. Moved repetitive evidence-boundary text into accessible native HTML details elements, preserving all original facts and distinctions.
7. Improved mobile navigation to retain discoverability of sections; retained all nine individual cases and the four team examples.
8. Linked historical V3.3 and older versions from the footer; all pre-existing paths remain intact.

## Evidence integrity
- Professional title and chronology unchanged; no unrelated e-commerce operations.
- No invented commercial KPIs, launch, customer validation, A/B test, private data, operational ownership, hiring outcome or paid research role.
- Paid education work is analytically relevant but is not recategorised as market analyst employment.
- Pathfinder: positive feedback and founder-level content planning, **not launched** in internship.
- Myprotein: 10 Korean / 7 UK purposively selected product references. This is not a UK market census or proof of demand.
- Lidl/FitFlop: original project methods and interpretation limits retained.
- Team academic outputs remain distinguished from personal contribution.
