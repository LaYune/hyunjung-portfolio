02 · Voluntary internship · 2026

Pathfinder audience insight and brand-search ambiguity

During my voluntary internship at The Pathfinder Organisation, I reviewed UK Google Trends data and found that searches for “Pathfinder” were mixed with unrelated gaming and automotive interests. This meant the broad brand search did not accurately reflect the intended audience.

  • I independently prepared an insight report recommending more targeted international-student-related search terms.
  • I also proposed a three-colour storytelling framework to support content planning: connection, adaptation and opportunity.
  • The storytelling proposal informed follow-up Instagram concept planning with the founder.

03 · Independent project · 2026

Lidl GB Power BI market overview

I independently built a Power BI dashboard to examine Lidl’s position in the UK grocery market. I selected the analytical questions, prepared public data using Excel and Power Query, and built the dashboard using Power BI and DAX.

  • The project focused on market share, competitor comparison, switching behaviour and store expansion direction.
  • Customer insight notes highlighted Lidl’s 8.6% market share and its competitive proximity to Aldi.
  • I used AI as a troubleshooting aid for technical concepts, but I made the decisions about comparisons, measures and presentation myself.
Power BI Power Query DAX

Evidence and scope

The original dashboard and supporting note were produced as an independent project rather than client work commissioned by Lidl. The project demonstrates analytical structuring and dashboard construction.

04 · Independent project · 2026

FitFlop UK Power BI portfolio

I developed a two-page Power BI portfolio focused on FitFlop UK. The first page reviewed publicly observable product and pricing structure, while the second page explored relative UK branded-search interest and competitor positioning over time.

  • This project helped me think carefully about what public data can and cannot support.
  • Search interest can suggest visibility or comparative attention, but it should not be treated as direct sales evidence.
  • The work demonstrates page structure, question selection and interpretation discipline in addition to the technical build.

Evidence and scope

The project was built independently. It is presented as a portfolio exercise rather than a commercial brand engagement.

How I approach work

My default workflow is to move from data or evidence to diagnosis, then to insight, recommendation and communication.

Question first

I start by clarifying the real question, not just the requested output. This helps reduce generic analysis and keeps the work decision-relevant.

Evidence with limits

I look for what the evidence genuinely supports, and I try to separate observation from interpretation before making recommendations.

Explain for use

I aim to present findings in a way that other people can use, whether that means a dashboard, a summary note or practical next steps.
