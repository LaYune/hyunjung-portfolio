UK voluntary internship · Feb–May 2026

The Pathfinder Organisation CIC

Audience and brand-search interpretation

Google TrendsDesk researchStakeholder planning

The question

Does interest in “Pathfinder” tell us anything reliable about prospective international students?

My action

Independently researched UK Google Trends (2021–2026) and the organisation’s brand/social content, then wrote and submitted an audience insight report.

What I identified

The name overlapped with unrelated gaming and automotive interest. A broad brand-search signal was not a clean proxy for the intended audience.

Recommendation

Use more specific international-student keywords and a three-colour narrative for connection, adaptation and opportunity.

What followed

Co-planned Instagram content ideas with the founder. Founder and Marketing Manager feedback was positive; the ideas were not launched during the internship.

Related: separate student interview project ↘
Evidence boundary

Internal planning is confirmed. An increase in traffic, followers, reach or sales is not claimed. The separate “What Is Home?” research involved at least 10 face-to-face international-student interviews.

02
Independent analytical portfolio · Oct 2026

Lidl GB

UK grocery comparisons in Power BI

Power BIPower QueryDAX

The question

Which comparisons distinguish Lidl’s market position from its potential drivers?

My action

Chose the analytical questions, KPIs and visual priorities; built a dashboard from public UK grocery evidence using Excel, Power Query, Power BI and DAX.

Comparison logic

Selected market share, competitor gaps, switching and proposed store expansion as different lenses rather than treating them as interchangeable measures.

Analytical judgement

Source, market definition and reporting period matter. A market-share difference in percentage points is not the same as relative percentage growth.

Actual output

An independent Power BI portfolio and interpretation framework. Public inputs, not internal Lidl customer or sales data.

Evidence boundary

No Lidl commission, deployed recommendation or commercial uplift is claimed. The V2 website demonstration uses illustrative figures, not the project's original raw market data.

03
Paid professional employment · South Korea

Lingua & Kookje

Performance diagnosis and stakeholder follow-up

Performance recordsKPI reportingRecommendations

The question

What was holding different learners back, and how should the next action change?

My action

Compared session results, earlier exams, class averages and question-level errors; diagnosed recurring gaps, communicated tailored next steps and reviewed later performance.

Method

Structured individual progress by Reading, Listening, Speaking, Writing and chapter-level weaknesses to distinguish needs within shared classes.

Stakeholder work

Explained findings to learners, families and internal staff. At Kookje, prepared KPI-related reporting and recommendations for management/clients.

Observed measures

At Lingua, supported approximately 50 learner/family stakeholders with around 95–100% retention in the assigned portfolio. Kookje’s assigned area recorded approximately 15% more new enrolments over one year.

Evidence boundary

Both positions were Teacher, Faculty Affairs Office, not business analyst roles. Metrics are restricted to their stated portfolios/areas and do not establish individual causation. The precise employer for the skill/chapter-level method still needs confirmation.

04 / My working method

A useful answer is more than a dashboard.

My strongest working pattern is structured, independently owned analysis followed by clear stakeholder discussion. I separate observed results from what still needs testing.

01

Define

What decision is being made? Which measure represents it?

02

Examine

03

04

Recommend

05

Check

06 / Transparency

What this portfolio does and does not show.

These boundaries are intentional. A credible case study says what is known, what is proposed and what would need to be verified.

Are these commercial client engagements?

No. Lidl and FitFlop are candidate-initiated portfolios using public data. IBM, L’Oréal and Innisfree are team/brand-strategy proposals. MSc research is academic. Pathfinder was a UK voluntary internship. None of these is presented as a paid brand consultancy engagement.

Are the percentages and budgets achieved results?

The approximately 95–100% Lingua retention is an observation within an assigned learner/family portfolio, not organisation-wide proof of cause. Kookje’s approximately 15% annual new-enrolment rise relates to the assigned area and is not solely attributed to the candidate. The IBM £400,000 engagement budget is a team proposal, not money spent. MODCASE’s commercial projections are assumptions, not sales.

Can online search data establish market performance?

Not by itself. Google Trends numbers are normalised indices of relative search interest, not units sold, revenue or conversion. Public market reports and retailer figures must be compared only with compatible dates, sources and market definitions.

Are the linked demonstrations using live company data?

No. The optional V2 market-share website explorer and commercial KPI exercise use illustrative data. They are teaching/demonstration interfaces, not an authorised company dashboard. Private client data and confidential business materials are not published here.
