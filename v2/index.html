03 / HOW A COMPANY SELECTS & USES KPIs

From a number to a commercial decision.

The company-side scenario below is hypothetical. It demonstrates my recommended method for translating a business objective into KPI definitions, diagnosis, experimentation and action. It is not a claim that I ran this initiative for Lidl or had access to its internal data.

Outcome · lagging

Incremental contribution profit

Incremental transactions × basket value × contribution margin, less incremental costs; compare against a valid counterfactual, not simply last week.

Drivers · leading

Reach → trial → repeat

Penetration, shopping frequency, basket value and conversion, segmented by store, customer cohort and period.

Guardrails

Quality of growth

Availability, margin, cannibalisation, acquisition cost and retention, plus source completeness and period comparability.

A practical KPI decision tree

Purpose: profitable customer growth, not a larger dashboard.

Strategic objective
Increase incremental contribution profit
Final business outcome. Owner: commercial / finance.
Decompose performance
Buyers × Trips per buyer × Basket value
Build a driver tree to identify what actually changed.
Validate measures
Same time window, population, denominator and data grain
Reconcile source data before interpreting a competitive gap.
Decision
Invest, test, adjust or stop
Use holdout stores / matched groups and a pre-agreed profitability threshold.

Metric specification: a real workplace standard

Example definitions, not measured brand results.
Market share: retailer grocery sales ÷ total defined grocery-market sales over the same period, geography and source.
Customer penetration: unique buying households ÷ eligible households in the defined panel and lookback window.
Repeat rate: customers with qualifying repeat visit within 90 days ÷ eligible acquisition cohort, allowing full observation time.
Incremental contribution: estimated additional gross contribution after variable costs and campaign cost, relative to a credible control.
Operating standard: assign metric owner, dataset, refresh cadence, inclusion/exclusion rules, segmentation and action threshold.

04 / DELIVERY WORKFLOW

How this becomes an employer-ready analysis.

Agree the decision

Which business question, metric owner, segment, baseline, time horizon and minimum viable commercial gain?

Inspect the evidence

Source lineage, missing records, duplication, period alignment, market definitions and formula validation.

Diagnose drivers

Compare segment contribution, mix shifts, customer cohorts, external conditions and alternative explanations.

Recommend an action

Specify the test, expected outcome, cost, downside risk, decision owner and stop conditions.

Test incrementality

Use matched stores, holdouts or random assignment where feasible, rather than attributing correlation to a campaign.

Review and learn

Track guardrails and post-launch outcomes, explain uncertainty, and update the recommendation.
