01 · PAID EMPLOYMENT · SOUTH KOREA

Performance diagnosis that led to follow-up

Kookje and Lingua Language Institutes · Teacher, Faculty Affairs Office

Business question
Which recurring performance gaps needed attention, and how should they be communicated?
What I personally did
Compared exam results, class averages and error patterns. At Kookje, analysed monthly performance data and feedback and prepared KPI-based reports for senior management and clients. At Lingua, explained findings and targeted next steps to learners, families and internal teams, then followed up on later results.
Observed outcome
Kookje's assigned area recorded an approximately 15% increase in new enrolments over one year. This was an area-level observation, not an increase caused solely by me.
What this demonstratesAnalysing real operational evidence, explaining recurring issues to stakeholders and following up.
02 · VOLUNTARY UK INTERNSHIP · 2026

When search interest points to the wrong audience

The Pathfinder Organisation CIC · personally authored UK audience insight report

Business question
Did broad searches for “Pathfinder” represent interest from prospective international students?
What I personally did
Reviewed UK Google Trends (2021–2026) and the organisation's social content, then wrote and submitted an insight report.
Finding
The brand name overlapped with unrelated gaming and automotive search interests. Broad brand-search data therefore needed more careful interpretation.
Recommendation and status
Proposed narrower international-student keywords and a three-colour storytelling framework. Co-planned Instagram feed concepts with the founder; the proposals were well received but not launched during the internship.
What this demonstratesTurning a misleading metric into a specific audience recommendation and stakeholder discussion.
03 · SELF-BUILT POWER BI · OCT 2026

Making UK grocery competitor comparisons defensible

Lidl GB · independent public-data portfolio, not commissioned brand work

Analytical question
How does Lidl compare with UK grocery competitors, and which measures tell different parts of the story?
What I personally built
Used Excel, Power Query, Power BI and DAX to create a market overview. Selected comparisons of retailer share, competitor gaps, switching gains and planned store expansion.
Analytical judgement
Market shares and competitor gaps need consistent source, period and market definitions. A share difference is measured in percentage points, while switching and store-expansion indicators answer other questions.
Actual output
Completed a Power BI portfolio. No retailer implementation or sales effect is claimed. The interactive example below uses illustrative data, not the original Lidl source rows. Explore the interactive example ↓
What this demonstratesSelecting and building the right comparison, then being explicit about what the evidence can support.

04 / OPTIONAL KPI EXERCISE

When an improving metric still loses money.

This optional scenario shows how I would connect a business objective, KPI definition and a commercial decision. Every input is hypothetical, not a result from employer or brand data.

Open KPI decision framework and simulatorTest conversion, costs and commercial contribution
Outcome · lagging

Incremental contribution profit

Incremental transactions × basket value × contribution margin, less incremental costs; compare against a valid counterfactual, not simply last week.

Drivers · leading

Reach → trial → repeat

Penetration, shopping frequency, basket value and conversion, segmented by store, customer cohort and period.

Guardrails

Quality of growth

Availability, margin, cannibalisation, acquisition cost and retention, plus source completeness and period comparability.

A practical KPI decision tree

Purpose: profitable customer growth, not a larger dashboard.

Strategic objective
Increase incremental contribution profit
Final business outcome. Owner: commercial / finance.
Decompose performance
Buyers × Trips per buyer × Basket value
Build a driver tree to identify what actually changed.
Validate measures
Same time window, population, denominator and data grain
Reconcile source data before interpreting a competitive gap.
Decision
Invest, test, adjust or stop
Use holdout stores / matched groups and a pre-agreed profitability threshold.

Metric specification: a real workplace standard

Example definitions, not measured brand results.
Market share: retailer grocery sales ÷ total defined grocery-market sales over the same period, geography and source.
Customer penetration: unique buying households ÷ eligible households in the defined panel and lookback window.
Repeat rate: customers with qualifying repeat visit within 90 days ÷ eligible acquisition cohort, allowing full observation time.
Incremental contribution: estimated additional gross contribution after variable costs and campaign cost, relative to a credible control.
Operating standard: assign metric owner, dataset, refresh cadence, inclusion/exclusion rules, segmentation and action threshold.
